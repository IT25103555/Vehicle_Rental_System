Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to browse vehicles?+

No — anyone can browse the fleet and check prices. You'll need a free account to confirm a booking.

How is the total rental cost calculated?+

We multiply the vehicle's daily rate by the number of rental days you select, shown before you confirm.

Can I cancel a booking?+

Yes, from the "My Bookings" page. Cancelling immediately frees the vehicle up for other customers.

Is payment on this site real?+

This academic project uses a simulated payment step — no real card details or transactions are processed.
